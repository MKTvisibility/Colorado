Idaho Springs, Colorado

Historic mining, geothermal soaking and mountain-town experiences west of Denver.

Indian Hot Springs

A historic geothermal destination with a mineral-water swimming pool, private baths, outdoor tubs and geothermal caves, plus spa services and lodging.

Address: 302 Soda Creek Road, Idaho Springs, CO 80452
Phone: 303-989-6666
Email: info@indianhotsprings.com

Argo Mill & Tunnel

A major Idaho Springs mining-history landmark where visitors can learn how ore from Colorado's mining districts was transported and processed. The preserved mill and tunnel connect the town directly to the story of Colorado's gold-mining era.

Address: 2060 Miner Street, Idaho Springs, CO 80452
Phone: 303-567-4382
