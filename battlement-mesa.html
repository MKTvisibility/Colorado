A Historic Schoolhouse and a Community Shaped by Oil Shale

The 1897 sandstone schoolhouse reflects the area’s rural past. Decades later, a planned community grew on the mesa during the oil-shale boom, adding another chapter to the history of this part of Garfield County.

Local History

A Brief History of Battlement Mesa

Battlement Mesa's modern community was developed by Exxon to house people connected with the anticipated oil-shale boom. The company's cancellation of the Colony project in 1982 abruptly changed that future. Rather than disappearing with the project, the planned community evolved into a residential development, illustrating the Western Slope's cycles of energy development and reinvention.
