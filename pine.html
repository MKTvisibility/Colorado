Pine, Colorado

Granite cliffs, forests, waterfalls and mountain recreation close to the Front Range.

Staunton State Park

A nearly 4,000-acre mountain park with grassy meadows, forested hillsides and granite cliffs rising above 10,000 feet. Its recreation includes hiking, mountain biking, rock climbing, geocaching and camping, and trails lead toward scenic features including Elk Falls.

Address: 12102 S. Elk Creek Road, Pine, CO 80470
Phone: 303-816-0912
