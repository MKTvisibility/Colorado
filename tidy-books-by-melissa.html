About Tidy Books by Melissa

Tidy Books by Melissa provides bookkeeping support for small businesses and individuals, helping clients maintain accurate and organized financial records. Services include ongoing monthly bookkeeping, financial reporting, catch-up and cleanup bookkeeping, and QuickBooks support. The business also partners with accountants and tax preparers.
