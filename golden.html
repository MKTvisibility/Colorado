Mountain trails, railroad history and classic Front Range scenery.

Golden Gate Canyon State Park

More than 12,000 acres of forests, rocky peaks and mountain meadows with over 35 miles of trails. Activities include hiking, biking, fishing, horseback riding, camping and rock climbing, while Panorama Point offers broad Continental Divide views.

Address: 92 Crawford Gulch Road, Golden, CO 80403
Phone: 303-582-3707

Colorado Railroad Museum

A 15-acre railyard with more than 100 historic locomotives, passenger cars and cabooses, plus a roundhouse, depot museum, model railroad and scheduled train experiences.

Address: 17155 W. 44th Avenue, Golden, CO 80403
Phone: 303-279-4591
Email: info@crrm.org
