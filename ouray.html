Ouray, Colorado

Hot mineral water, canyon walls, waterfalls and high-country adventure.

Ouray Hot Springs Pool

A family-friendly, sulfur-free geothermal pool complex surrounded by the San Juan Mountains. The facility operates year-round, with soaking areas and seasonal recreation features.

Address: 1220 Main Street, Ouray, CO 81427
Phone: 970-325-7073

Box Cañon Falls Park

A City of Ouray park centered on the dramatic Box Cañon waterfall and narrow canyon. Trails and viewing areas make the gorge one of the community's signature natural attractions.
