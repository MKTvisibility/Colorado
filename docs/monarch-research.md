# Monarch research

Next directory entry after Moffat. No matching Monarch filename in the current main tree. Distinguish the historic Monarch mining settlement (originally Chaffee City), nearby Garfield, and the later Monarch Mountain ski area. A guide can explain the distinct places and label the map accordingly.

- [Chaffee County Historic Resource Survey, 2013](https://garna.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/Chaffee-County-Historic-Resource-Survey-Report-2013.pdf) identifies the historic Monarch settlement, its 1880s mining boom, businesses and newspaper, and describes Garfield separately.
- [Salida Digital Archive, Donna Nevens Collection](https://digital.salidalibrary.org/donna-nevens-collection/) locates the historic settlement west of Garfield and documents the mining district.
- [Monarch Mountain Master Development Plan](https://skimonarch.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/monarch-mountain-mdp-august-2011.pdf) documents the separate ski area and its lifts. Read the relevant plan sections before including resort dates.
