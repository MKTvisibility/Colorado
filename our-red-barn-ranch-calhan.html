Calhan • Agritourism

Our Red Barn Ranch

A working Eastern Plains ranch where visitors can meet alpacas and other animals, learn about fiber, ride horses and experience a little of the farm-and-ranch way of life.

Address: 11494 N Calhan Hwy, Calhan, CO 80808
Phone: (719) 339-5229
Email: ourredbarnranch@gmail.com
Website: ourredbarnranch.com
Visits: By appointment

Why MKT Colorado stopped here

Our Red Barn Ranch began raising alpacas in 2003 and now shares ranch life with visitors through interactive tours, hands-on animal experiences, horseback riding, fiber processing and a farm stand. The ranch also offers RV stays, making it an unusual overnight option near Paint Mines Interpretive Park.

Pair it with the Paint Mines

The ranch is only a few miles from the Paint Mines area, which makes the two experiences an easy combination: hike the formations in the cooler morning hours, eat in Calhan, then schedule a ranch visit for later in the day.
