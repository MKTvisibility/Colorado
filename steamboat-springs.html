Steamboat Springs, Colorado

Natural hot springs, mountain recreation and a classic Colorado alpine slide.

Strawberry Park Hot Springs

Natural stone-lined thermal pools in a forested mountain setting outside downtown Steamboat Springs. The springs are open year-round and reservations are required; winter vehicle requirements apply from November through April.

Address: 44200 County Road 36, Steamboat Springs, CO 80487
Phone: 970-879-0342
Email: info@strawberryhotsprings.com

The Howler Alpine Slide

A 2,400-foot alpine slide on historic Howelsen Hill. Riders take a scenic chairlift uphill, then control their own sled down the winding summer track. The 2026 season operates on selected dates through September 20, weather permitting.

Address: 645 Howelsen Parkway, Steamboat Springs, CO 80487
Phone: 970-439-0863
Email: alpineslide@sswsc.org
