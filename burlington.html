Eastern Plains • Kit Carson County

Burlington, Colorado

Colorado's eastern gateway is much more than an I-70 stop. Burlington combines remarkable prairie history, one-of-a-kind attractions, locally owned food and shopping, and family-friendly places worth leaving the interstate to discover.

Discover Burlington

A small Colorado city with some very big stories

Established along the railroad in 1888, Burlington became the Kit Carson County seat the following year and grew into the trade center for the surrounding agricultural region. Today its location near the Kansas border makes it one of Colorado's first welcomes for westbound travelers and one of the state's last stops heading east.

Its standout attraction arrived in 1928, when Kit Carson County purchased a 1905 Philadelphia Toboggan Company carousel from Denver's Elitch Gardens. The purchase became a local political story, but the carousel survived, was restored, and today remains fully operational.

Burlington Spotlight

Kit Carson County Carousel & Museum

This fully operating 1905 Philadelphia Toboggan Company carousel is a National Historic Landmark and one of Burlington's signature treasures. Its hand-carved menagerie animals and scenery retain original paint, accompanied by a historic Wurlitzer Monster Military Band Organ. The carousel came to Kit Carson County from Elitch Gardens in 1928 for $1,200—a purchase the City says ultimately cost the commissioners their jobs.

Location: Kit Carson County Fairgrounds, 815 15th Street, Burlington, CO 80807
Season: Memorial Day–Labor Day, 11 a.m.–6 p.m.
Current published price: $1 rides; $2 museum admission.

Things To Do

Worth Getting Off I-70 For

History, unusual roadside stops and family-friendly places that give Burlington its own personality.

Living History

Old Town Museum

Explore 6½ acres with 21 restored turn-of-the-century buildings and more than 25,000 artifacts. Summer Saturdays bring High Noon can-can shows and live gunfights, while the Emporium offers hand-dipped ice cream, gifts and Colorado souvenirs.

420 S 14th St
(719) 346-7382 / (800) 288-1334

Family Fun

Parmer Park — “Spaceship Park”

A memorable road-trip break with a large space-themed playground, splash park, picnic areas and amphitheater. Its rocket-style play structures make it one of Burlington's most distinctive family stops.

1858 Ben St
(719) 346-8652

Welcome to Colorful Colorado

Events & Traditions

Come Back for the Experience

Kit Carson County Fair

The annual county fair is typically held in late July at the Kit Carson County Fairgrounds, bringing together livestock and 4-H activities, rodeo, arts and crafts, carnival attractions and the historic carousel.

Old Town Summer & Seasonal Events

Old Town Museum hosts live can-can shows and gunfights during the summer season along with special events such as Wild West Dinner Theatre. Check the museum's official calendar before traveling for a specific event.
