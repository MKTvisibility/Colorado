Meet The Legend

High-Alpine Colorado at Its Boldest

Arapahoe Basin is not an incorporated Colorado town. It is a mountain destination and ski area on U.S. Highway 6 in Summit County, near Loveland Pass and the Continental Divide. MKT Colorado includes it in the A–Z guide because A-Basin has a distinct identity, history and visitor culture of its own.

Founded in 1946 by Larry Jump and Sandy Schauffler, both former ski racers and World War II 10th Mountain Division veterans, A-Basin opened with a single rope tow. It is the oldest ski area in Summit County and in 2026 celebrates 80 years on the Divide.

What Arapahoe Basin Is Known For

Steep skiing, high elevation and a fiercely distinctive culture. The East Wall, Pallavicini, The Steep Gullies, The Beavers, Montezuma Bowl, spring skiing and the famous base-area “Beach” have made A-Basin one of Colorado’s most recognizable ski destinations.

Mountain History

80 Years on the Continental Divide

A-Basin’s story began as a shoestring postwar ski project. Through changing ownership, new lifts and terrain expansions, the mountain developed a reputation for serious skiing without losing its informal character. The Pallavicini lift arrived in 1978, and later expansions added Montezuma Bowl, The Beavers and The Steep Gullies.

A-Basin was also an early Colorado home for snowboarding. In recent decades the ski area expanded beyond winter with hiking, mountain biking, disc golf and a high-alpine via ferrata. Alterra Mountain Company purchased Arapahoe Basin in 2024.

A-Basin Character

Symbols That Tell the Story

🏔️

The East Wall

Dramatic above-treeline terrain is one of the mountain’s signature sights and a defining part of its expert reputation.

⛷️

Pallavicini

The legendary Pali terrain and lift represent the steep, technical skiing that generations of A-Basin regulars love.

🚙

The Beach

Front-row base-area parking spaces are famous for tailgating, grilling and a social scene unlike a conventional ski lodge.

❄️

Long Seasons

High elevation and snowmaking help A-Basin pursue one of Colorado’s longest ski seasons, with memorable spring skiing.

🧗

Summer Adventure

Warm-weather offerings include hiking, biking, disc golf and a high-alpine via ferrata.

🎿

Come-As-You-Are Culture

A-Basin has built its identity around passionate skiers and riders, community traditions and a relaxed, no-frills mountain spirit.
