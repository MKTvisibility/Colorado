Elbert County • Colorado High Plains

Elbert, Colorado

A quiet historic community shaped by railroad days, ranching, farming, Kiowa Creek and buildings that survived one of the town’s defining disasters.

Why Elbert Belongs on the Colorado A–Z

Small-Town Colorado With a Remarkable Past

Settled around 1860 in the Kiowa Creek valley, the community was first known as Gomer’s Gulch. The arrival of the Denver & New Orleans Railroad in the 1880s helped Elbert grow into a busy rural trade center. A catastrophic 1935 flood changed the town forever, but several historic buildings remain and help tell its story today.

Local History

Railroad Growth, Mercantile Commerce & the 1935 Flood

Elbert grew after the Denver & New Orleans Railroad established a route through the community in the 1880s. At its height, the town supported multiple mercantile stores, banks, hotels, creameries and other businesses serving surrounding farms and ranches.

On Memorial Day weekend in 1935, extraordinary rainfall caused Kiowa Creek to flood, destroying homes and businesses and damaging the railroad so severely that the line was abandoned. Historic survivors include St. Mark United Presbyterian Church, the Russell Gates Mercantile and Sacred Heart Church, which was later moved to higher ground.

What Elbert Is Known For

Elbert is a living piece of Colorado plains history. Its identity combines ranch and farm country, historic railroad roots, the Russell Gates Mercantile, surviving churches, antiques and small independent businesses.

Elbert Character

Symbols That Tell the Story

🏪 Russell Gates Mercantile

Built in 1906, the landmark mercantile once served as the center of Russell Gates’ regional retail enterprise and remains an important community building.

🚂 Railroad Heritage

The Denver & New Orleans Railroad helped transform Elbert from a small settlement into a thriving rural trade center.

🌾 Ranch & Farm Country

Open prairie, livestock, agricultural land and rural traditions remain central to the character of the Elbert area.

⛪ Historic Churches

St. Mark United Presbyterian Church dates to 1889, while Sacred Heart Church survived the 1935 flood and was moved to higher ground.

🌊 Kiowa Creek

The creek shaped Elbert’s settlement and its history, most dramatically during the devastating flood of 1935.

🪵 Antiques & Western Finds

Elbert’s small-town shopping includes antiques, reclaimed materials, western-inspired goods and locally made products.
