Estes Park, Colorado

Rocky Mountain scenery, historic destinations and memorable ways to see the high country.

PRIMARY ESTES PARK FEATURE
Memories Old Time Portraits

Memories Old Time Portraits

A family-owned Estes Park tradition creating interactive Old West portraits for more than 30 years. Visitors dress in period costumes and leave with a one-of-a-kind Colorado vacation memory. Large groups and pets are welcome.

Address: 132 W Elkhorn Ave, Estes Park, CO 80517
Phone: (970) 586-5568

Official website →
Mustang Mountain Coaster

Mustang Mountain Coaster

An alpine coaster through historic Sombrero Ranch, giving visitors a fast, scenic “horseless trail ride” through the ponderosa pines.

Address: 1180 Dry Gulch Rd, Estes Park, CO 80517
Phone: (970) 672-1829

Official website →
Sombrero Stables

Sombrero Stables

A multi-generational Western riding operation established in 1958, offering guided Rocky Mountain trail rides on specially trained horses.

Address: 1895 Big Thompson Ave, Estes Park, CO 80517
Phone: (970) 533-8155

Official website →
National Park Gateway Stables

National Park Gateway Stables

Guided horseback rides into Rocky Mountain National Park from just outside the Fall River entrance, with trips ranging from shorter scenic rides to longer mountain adventures.

Address: 4600 Fall River Rd, Estes Park, CO 80517
Phone: (970) 586-5269

Official website →
Fun City

Fun City

A longtime family amusement park with a spinning roller coaster, go-karts, mini golf, bumper boats, gemstone panning, bungee trampoline and more.

Address: 455 Prospect Village Dr, Estes Park, CO 80517
Phone: (970) 586-2828

Official website →
Ride-A-Kart

Ride-A-Kart & Cascade Creek Mini Golf

An Estes Park family entertainment destination operating for more than 60 years, with go-karts, mini golf, bumper boats, arcade games, a miniature railroad and other activities.

Address: 2250 Big Thompson Ave, Estes Park, CO 80517
Phone: (970) 586-6495

Official website →
WildSide 4x4 Tours

WildSide 4x4 Tours

Guided Rocky Mountain National Park sightseeing and true off-road adventures in Roosevelt National Forest, including photography-focused and high-adventure routes.

Address: 212 E Elkhorn Ave, Estes Park, CO 80517
Phone: (970) 586-8687

Official website →
Red Rose Rock Shop & Dick's Rock Museum

Red Rose Rock Shop & Dick's Rock Museum

A rock, mineral and fossil shop with roots dating to 1939, paired with a free historic rock museum featuring an eclectic collection of minerals, fossils and lapidary displays.

Address: 490 Moraine Ave, Estes Park, CO 80517
Phone: (970) 586-4180

Official website →
The Ore Cart Rock Shop

The Ore Cart Rock Shop

A family-run Estes Park rock shop in the same downtown location since 1972, specializing in distinctive gifts made from the natural beauty of stone.

Address: 119 W Elkhorn Ave, Estes Park, CO 80517
Phone: (970) 586-3577

Official website →
The Taffy Shop

The Taffy Shop

An Estes Park institution making salt water taffy in the same location since 1935. Visitors can sometimes watch the vintage Model K Kiss wrapping machine in action.

Address: 121 W Elkhorn Ave, Estes Park, CO 80517
Phone: (970) 586-4548

Official website →
Macdonald Book Shop

Macdonald Book Shop

An independent downtown bookstore established in 1928, with a strong selection of books by local authors and titles about Colorado and the Rocky Mountains.

Address: 152 E Elkhorn Ave, Estes Park, CO 80517
Phone: (970) 586-3450

Official website →
Trendz at the Park

Trendz at the Park

A large downtown boutique known for an eclectic collection of contemporary mountain home décor, artwork, jewelry and gifts, including Colorado and American-made goods.
