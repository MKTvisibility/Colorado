Estes Park, Colorado

Rocky Mountain scenery, historic destinations and memorable ways to see the high country.

Rocky Mountain National Park

One of Colorado's signature national parks, encompassing 415 square miles of mountain environments, more than 300 miles of hiking trails, alpine lakes, wildlife habitat and high-elevation scenery. Trail Ridge Road and many park facilities are seasonal, so current NPS conditions should be checked before visiting.

Gateway communities: Estes Park and Grand Lake

The Stanley Hotel

The landmark 1909 hotel offers public historic, ghost, Shining-themed and other guided tours that explore its architecture, history, folklore and pop-culture connections.

Address: 333 E Wonderview Ave, Estes Park, CO 80517
Phone: (970) 577-4000
Email: info@stanleyhotel.com

Estes Park Aerial Tram

A seasonal scenic tramway to the summit of Prospect Mountain, with panoramic views toward Estes Park, Rocky Mountain National Park, Longs Peak and the Continental Divide. The official 2026 season runs May 9 through November 1, weather permitting.

Address: 420 E Riverside Dr, Estes Park, CO
