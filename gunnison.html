Curecanti National Recreation Area

Three reservoirs and surrounding public lands form this National Park Service recreation area. Visitors can boat, fish, camp, hike, explore geology and enjoy both land- and water-based recreation. Blue Mesa Reservoir is the largest of the three reservoirs; seasonal water levels, boating access and facility operations should be checked before a trip.
