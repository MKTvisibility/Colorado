Littleton & Roxborough

Reservoir recreation and dramatic red-rock landscapes southwest of Denver.

Chatfield State Park

A major Front Range recreation destination centered on Chatfield Reservoir and the South Platte River. Visitors come for boating, camping, trails, cycling, paddling, fishing, wildlife viewing and other land-and-water recreation.

Address: 11500 N. Roxborough Park Rd., Littleton, CO 80125
Phone: 303-791-7275

Roxborough State Park

A 3,413-acre natural area known for dramatic red-rock formations, wildlife and about 15 miles of trails. The park emphasizes hiking, scenery and natural-resource protection rather than developed recreation.

Address: 4751 East Roxborough Drive, Roxborough, CO 80125
Phone: 303-973-3959
