Cañon City, Colorado

Royal Gorge scenery and high-adrenaline adventure above the Arkansas River.

Royal Gorge Bridge & Park

A year-round attraction centered on the suspension bridge spanning the Royal Gorge 956 feet above the Arkansas River. General admission includes the bridge and available core attractions, while additional experiences include the Cloudscraper Zip Line, Royal Rush Skycoaster and Via Ferrata. Rides can close for weather or maintenance.

Address: 4218 County Road 3A, Cañon City, CO 81212
