Why MKT Colorado?

Because Colorado's character lives in its communities, independent businesses, makers, farms, attractions and stories.

A Statewide Place to Discover Local

MKT Colorado is being built town by town as a statewide discovery guide. Our goal is not to become another giant list of everything. We focus on locally rooted businesses, memorable places, independent makers and experiences that give people a reason to explore Colorado communities.

Our approach: discover what is genuinely local, verify useful information, tell the story behind it, and make it easier for people to support Colorado businesses.

What We Feature

Businesses

Independent shops, restaurants, services, farms and locally rooted destinations.

Places

Attractions, recreation, cultural destinations and experiences worth discovering.

Stories

The people, history, ideas and passion that make a Colorado place memorable.

Presented by MKT Visibility

MKT Colorado is a statewide project presented by MKT Visibility. The directory gives Colorado businesses a place to be discovered. Businesses that want additional help can work with MKT Visibility for social media, websites, digital marketing, photography, video and Google Business Profile support.
