Our Story

Why MKT Colorado

MKT Colorado began in 2026 as an idea to create one place where people could discover and share the things that make communities across Colorado worth exploring.

In September 2026, an unexpected corporate layoff changed my direction. It gave me the desire to take what I had learned throughout my working years and use that experience to help small businesses. Owning a business and working for myself had also been a dream of mine for a long time.

Instead of simply moving on to another corporate position, I partnered with my son Tim Skolyak and Kayli Elliott to start MKT Visibility, a family-owned marketing business focused on helping small businesses get noticed, connect with customers and grow.

MKT Colorado grew from that same belief: communities are stronger when local businesses are visible, people support one another and residents and visitors have an easy way to discover what makes each Colorado town unique.

Community + Local Business

Why These Two Projects Belong Together

The world can feel pretty complicated sometimes, but one thing we can influence is what happens right here in our own communities.

I believe communities are stronger when local businesses thrive, neighbors support one another, and people get out and experience the places around them.

That's where MKT Colorado and MKT Visibility work together.

MKT Colorado

MKT Colorado is for the community. It's a place to discover local businesses, restaurants, attractions, activities, events, history, parks and the people and stories that make communities throughout Colorado unique.

MKT Visibility

MKT Visibility is for the businesses behind those communities. We help small businesses become easier to find and give owners support with marketing, websites, social media, content, photography and their online presence.

For residents and visitors, we hope you'll use this site to discover more of Colorado and support the people and businesses you find along the way.

For business owners, we hope MKT Colorado gives you another place to be discovered—and if you need more help getting your business seen, that's where MKT Visibility comes in.
