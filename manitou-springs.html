Manitou Springs, Colorado

Caves, cliff dwellings, Victorian architecture and mountain adventure at the foot of Pikes Peak.

Cave of the Winds Mountain Park

A year-round show-cave destination with multiple cave tours plus weather-dependent above-ground adventures including the Terror-dactyl, via ferrata and zip lines.

Address: 100 Cave of the Winds Rd, Manitou Springs, CO 80829
Phone: (719) 685-5444
Email: info@caveofthewinds.com

Manitou Cliff Dwellings

A visitor attraction where guests can walk through and touch reconstructed Ancestral Puebloan-style cliff dwellings and explore two museums with cultural displays. Open year-round on a seasonal schedule, weather permitting.

Address: 10 Cliff Rd, Manitou Springs, CO 80829
Phone: (719) 685-5242

Official website →

Miramont Castle

Operated by the Manitou Springs Historical Society, this distinctive historic property offers self-guided tours through 30 rooms, period furnishings, exhibits and seasonal gardens.

Address: 9 Capitol Hill Ave, Manitou Springs, CO 80829
Phone: 719-685-1011
Email: miramontcastle@yahoo.com
