Sunflower Farm

A Longmont farm where children and families can reconnect with animals, nature, imaginative play and the rhythms of farm life.

Sunflower Farm • Photo supplied for MKT Colorado Spotlight

A Longmont place built for discovery

Sunflower Farm is a 55-acre farm in Longmont where education, animals, nature and old-fashioned outdoor play come together. The farm describes its mission as cultivating an appreciation for farming, nature and a sustainable future through education, daily farm routines, experiential play and family time.

The farm has been part of the community for more than two decades. In 2018, it was officially designated an Educational Demonstration Farm, supporting its licensed programs and opportunities for families to experience the property.

Sometimes the best local places are the ones that give families room to slow down, get a little dirty and make a memory together.

Why Sunflower Farm is In The Spotlight

Sunflower Farm is more than a place to look at farm animals. Its public Farmfest experiences invite families to explore the property, interact directly with animals and let children discover the farm at their own pace.

Visitors may meet goats, sheep, llamas, cows, alpacas, chickens and horses. Around the farm are places for children to climb hay bales, play in sandboxes and playhouses, swing beneath the cottonwoods, explore tree forts, sit on old tractors and discover unexpected features such as an old airplane.

Farmfest and family time

Public Farmfest dates are scheduled throughout the year rather than operating as ordinary daily business hours. Reservations are required because attendance is capacity controlled. Seasonal experiences change with the farm and weather; autumn can bring the pumpkin patch and Magical Halloween Forest, while warmer days can include water play.

The farm also hosts Farmfest Music Evenings during the warmer season, combining live music with access to farm activities and animals. Food trucks are part of the music-evening experience on scheduled dates.

Learning through farm life

Education is central to Sunflower Farm. Its licensed programs include preschool, pre-kindergarten and a primarily outdoor school-age program. Children use the farm itself as a learning environment, with experiences that can include animal care, planting, harvesting, nature-based art and hands-on farm chores.

Summer programming extends that approach into camps designed around animals, gardening, art and active outdoor play.

Connected to the community

Sunflower Farm also uses its Farmfest setting for community outreach. The farm has hosted fundraisers supporting local nonprofits, offers Farmfest reservation donations for school and organization auctions, and provides teen and adult volunteer opportunities.

Planning your visit

Sunflower Farm is not a drop-in attraction every day. Public access is offered through scheduled Farmfests and other events, and advance reservations are required. Dates, hours, admission and seasonal activities can change, so visitors should check the farm's official website before making a special trip.
