Meet Antonito

A Town Built by the Railroad

Antonito owes its existence to the Denver & Rio Grande Railroad. As the railroad pushed south from Alamosa in 1880, company officials established their own townsite near the older community of Conejos. The settlement was first associated with the name San Antonio Junction; within months the name became Antonito—Spanish for “little Anthony.”

The first train reached the new town on March 27, 1880. Antonito quickly developed around the tracks with a depot, railroad facilities, stores, churches, warehouses and homes. Its position made it a transportation and shipping center for the southern San Luis Valley and northern New Mexico.

Local History

Narrow Gauge, Ranching & a Crossroads of the Southwest

Antonito became a major railroad junction. One route headed west across Cumbres Pass toward Chama, Durango and the San Juan mining country, while another ran south toward Santa Fe. The Santa Fe branch—later remembered as the Chili Line—closed in 1941, but the mountain route survived.

The surviving 64-mile narrow-gauge line from Antonito to Chama was originally built in 1880 as part of the Rio Grande’s San Juan Extension to serve southwestern Colorado’s silver-mining district. After regular freight operations ended, Colorado and New Mexico jointly purchased the scenic section in 1970. The Cumbres & Toltec Scenic Railroad began carrying tourists the following year and remains one of Antonito’s defining attractions.

Antonito’s historic railroad landscape includes its distinctive 1880 Denver & Rio Grande depot, built of local volcanic stone. Farming, sheep ranching and regional trade also shaped the community, while generations of Hispanic families have given the southern San Luis Valley its enduring cultural character.

What Antonito Is Known For

Antonito is one of Colorado’s great railroad towns. Historic steam locomotives, the Cumbres & Toltec Scenic Railroad, the old lava-stone depot, wide San Luis Valley landscapes and access to Conejos River Canyon give the community a character unlike anywhere else in the state.
