Eastern Plains • Elbert County

Agate, Colorado

A small Eastern Plains community with railroad and ranching roots, local producers and roadside history worth discovering.

Discover Agate

A tiny town with a story worth stopping for

Agate began as a railroad-era settlement in the 1870s and was first known as Gebhard. The community became Agate in 1882. The origin of Agate’s name has more than one historical explanation: one longstanding local story connects it to an “A-gate,” a wooden livestock gate reinforced with an A-shaped brace, while another account says the community was named for agate stone found in the area.

Agate Roadside History

Roadside Landmark

The Agate “A-Gate”

An A-braced gate near the center of town is connected to a longstanding local story about the origin of Agate’s name. Historical sources also record an alternative explanation that the town was named for agate stone found in the area.

Location: The I-70 Corridor Chamber places the gate near Main Street & 2nd Avenue, Agate, CO 80101.
