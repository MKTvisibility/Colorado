Eastern Plains • Elbert County

Agate, Colorado

A small Eastern Plains community with railroad and ranching roots, local producers and roadside history worth discovering.

Discover Agate

A tiny town with a story worth stopping for

Agate began as a railroad-era settlement in the 1870s and was first known as Gebhard. The community became Agate in 1882. Local history connects the name to an “A-gate,” a wooden livestock gate reinforced with an A-shaped brace.

Once a busier highway and rail stop, Agate is now a quiet ranching community where local agriculture and traces of its transportation history remain part of the landscape.
