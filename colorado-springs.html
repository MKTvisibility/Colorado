A Brief History of Colorado Springs

William Jackson Palmer founded Colorado Springs in 1871 with a vision of a resort community beneath Pikes Peak. The Palmers helped shape institutions and development throughout the area. The city's origin as a deliberately planned destination distinguishes its early history from neighboring settlements that grew primarily around mining camps.
