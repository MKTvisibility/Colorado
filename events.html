Traditions Worth Planning For

Colorado Annual Events & Festivals

Discover recurring festivals, fairs, rodeos, celebrations and Colorado traditions held year after year across the state.

Plan ahead: MKT Colorado focuses on recurring annual events rather than maintaining a day-to-day calendar. Months and seasons below are a planning guide. Dates, locations, admission and schedules can change each year, so always check the event's official website before making plans.

🤠 Western Heritage & Fairs

Rodeos, livestock, agriculture and traditions that celebrate Colorado's Western roots.

Denver • January

National Western Stock Show

A Colorado tradition since 1906 featuring livestock, rodeo, horse shows, agricultural education and Western heritage.

Official website →
Greeley • Late June / Early July

Greeley Stampede

Rodeos, concerts, carnival attractions, parades, food and family activities in a long-running Northern Colorado celebration.

Official website →
Pueblo • Late August / Early September

Colorado State Fair & Rodeo

Colorado's statewide fair brings together agriculture, livestock, carnival rides, concerts, exhibits, food and rodeo.

Official website →

🎵 Music & Arts

Annual gatherings built around live music, artists and Colorado's creative communities.

Telluride • June

Telluride Bluegrass Festival

A landmark annual music festival that brings performers and festivalgoers to Telluride each June.

Official website →
Colorado Springs • Memorial Day Weekend

MeadowGrass Music Festival

An annual Pikes Peak-region music festival held over Memorial Day weekend with live performances and a community-focused atmosphere.

Official website →
Crested Butte • Summer

Crested Butte Arts Festival

A long-running juried fine-art festival on Elk Avenue supporting artists and arts programs in the Gunnison Valley.

Official website →

🌶️ Food & Drink

Colorado flavors, agriculture and craft traditions take center stage at these recurring festivals.

Pueblo • September

Pueblo Chile & Frijoles Festival

A downtown celebration of Pueblo's agricultural heritage with chile roasting, local food, vendors, entertainment and competitions.

Official website →
Denver • Fall

Great American Beer Festival

A major annual craft-beverage festival and competition presented by the Brewers Association.

Official website →
Colorado Springs • Summer

Taste of Pikes Peak

An annual food-and-drink event highlighting chefs, restaurants, breweries and flavors from the Pikes Peak region.

Event information →

❄️ Winter & Mountain Traditions

Distinctly Colorado celebrations where snow, mountains and local traditions are part of the experience.

Steamboat Springs • February

Steamboat Winter Carnival

A century-old winter tradition celebrating Steamboat's Western heritage and winter-sports culture with unusual street events and festivities.

Official event information →
Cripple Creek • Winter

Cripple Creek Ice Festival

A recurring winter celebration known for elaborate ice sculptures and a mountain-town festival atmosphere.

Event information →
Manitou Springs • Fall

Emma Crawford Coffin Races & Festival

One of the Pikes Peak region's most unusual annual traditions, combining costumed teams, racing coffins and community celebration.

Event information →

🎈 Community & Colorado Traditions

Signature celebrations that have become part of the identity of their Colorado communities.

Estes Park • Fall

Elk Fest

A free annual celebration of the elk rut with wildlife education, exhibits, entertainment, vendors and the mountain setting of Estes Park.

Official event information →
Colorado Springs • Labor Day Weekend

Labor Day Lift Off

A signature Colorado Springs tradition featuring hot-air balloons and a colorful holiday-weekend celebration.

Event information →
Old Colorado City • Memorial Day Weekend

Territory Days

A long-running street festival with entertainment, vendors and activities celebrating the historic Old Colorado City community.
