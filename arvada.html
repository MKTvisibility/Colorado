Meet Arvada

Where Colorado Gold History Meets a Modern Main Street

Arvada's story reaches back to June 22, 1850, when Lewis Ralston found gold in the creek that now bears his name. The discovery is recognized by the Colorado Historical Society as Colorado's first documented gold discovery. The town was formally named and platted in 1870, and the historic heart of the community survives today as Olde Town Arvada.

Olde Town blends preserved historic character with independent shops, restaurants, galleries, breweries, cafés and community events. Beyond downtown, Arvada offers a major arts campus, nature centers, creek trails and wide-open Front Range views.

Local History

Gold, Farms, Railroads & Celery

After the early gold discovery, settlers established farms throughout the Ralston and Clear Creek valleys. Arvada became an important agricultural community supplying Denver and nearby mining camps with produce. Crops included wheat, corn, oats, berries, melons and vegetables, and the city was once known as the “celery capital of the world.”

Benjamin Franklin Wadsworth helped lay out the original nine-block townsite. His wife, Mary Ann, selected the name Arvada after her brother-in-law Hiram Arvada Haskins. The railroad later became another backbone of the growing community, a history still reflected around Olde Town.

What Arvada Is Known For

Arvada connects Colorado's earliest gold-rush history with one of the Front Range's most recognizable historic downtowns. Add a strong arts community, local food and drink, extensive trails, vintage automobiles, mountain views and easy access to Denver, and Arvada has an identity all its own.

Places to Know in Arvada

Olde Town Arvada

Arvada's historic downtown district combines turn-of-the-century character with shops, restaurants, galleries and events.

Visitor hub: 7307 Grandview Ave
Phone: (303) 420-4769
Website: oldetownarvada.org

Arvada Center for the Arts and Humanities

A major cultural campus with theatre, concerts, free art galleries, classes and arts programming.

Address: 6901 Wadsworth Blvd
Phone: (720) 898-7200
Website: arvadacenter.org

Majestic View Nature Center

An environmental education center connected to an 80-acre park with prairie, wetlands, trails, gardens and mountain views.

Address: 7030 Garrison St
Phone: (720) 898-7405

Cussler Museum

A seasonal museum preserving more than 100 rare and vintage automobiles collected by adventure novelist Clive Cussler.

Address: 14959 W 69th Ave
Phone: (303) 420-2795
Website: cusslermuseum.com

Gold Strike Park

At the confluence of Clear Creek and Ralston Creek, this historic site commemorates Colorado's first documented gold discovery.

Ralston Creek Trail

Arvada's longest and most complete public trail follows the creek through parks, open space, wildlife habitat and historic areas.
